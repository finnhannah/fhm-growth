Questions

How many estimates can I expect?

It depends on your service, your area and your ad budget. We’ll give you a realistic picture on the call, and from day one you see every lead and exactly what it cost.

Are the leads shared with other contractors?

No. Leads from your ads come only to you. Nobody else gets them.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. You don’t pay my fee until your first qualified lead comes in: a real person who clicks one of your Meta ads and fills out the estimate form. After that it’s month to month, and you can stop anytime.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly, from an ad account in your company’s name, so you see every dollar. My fee is separate, and we agree on it before anything starts.

Do I need a website?

No. The estimate form opens right inside Facebook and Instagram, so homeowners can send their details without leaving the app.

What do you need from me?

Photos of real jobs (phone pictures are fine), the areas you work in, and someone to call leads back. About an hour of your time to get set up.
